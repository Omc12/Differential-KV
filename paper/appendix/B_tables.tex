(\path{paper/scripts/run_clean.sh} and \path{measure_tail.sh}) for the two \mlxe{} engines,
exactly as recorded by the isolated workers; the PyTorch engine is measured by a separate worker
and appears only in Table~\ref{tab:main}. \emph{MLX peak} is the allocator peak

uniformly empty: the isolated workers did not persist the tokenizer count for the primary sweep,
and we leave the column blank rather than back-fill it from a later re-tokenisation. Failed cells
would be stated as failures in the text, never given an invented value.
